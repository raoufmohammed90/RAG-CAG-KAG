Annual leave policy

Owner: HR · Version 4.2

How much leave you get

Every full-time employee gets 22 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Part-time employees get leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

New joiners receive leave pro rata from their start date.

Public holidays do not count against annual leave.

Requesting leave

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.

Requests of more than 10 consecutive days need approval from your department head as well as your line manager.

Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried over into the next year.

Carried-over days expire if they are not used by 31 March of the next year.

A carry-over request must be approved by the employee's line manager before 15 December.

Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave.

A doctor's note is needed after 2 consecutive days of sick leave.
