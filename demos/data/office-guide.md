# Office guide

## Opening hours and access
Both offices are open from 7 am to 9 pm, Sunday to Thursday.
Staff badges must be worn and visible at all times inside the office.
Visitors sign in at reception and are escorted by the employee they are visiting.

## Working arrangements
Hybrid work allows up to 2 remote days per week, agreed with your line manager.
Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar app, for up to 2 hours at a time.

## Facilities
Free parking is available in the basement for employees with a registered car.
The kitchen is cleaned every evening; please label food kept in the fridge.
Fire drills happen twice a year, and the assembly point is the front car park.
