# Expenses policy

> Last edited by finance-bot on 2026-09-02   


## Claiming expenses   

Employees can claim business expenses through the finance portal within 30 days.   
Every claim needs a receipt; claims above 500 AED also need a short business reason.
Finance pays approved claims with the next monthly salary.



## Travel

Taxi and ride-hailing costs are covered for client visits and late work after 9 pm.
Flights must be booked in economy class unless the flight is longer than 6 hours.
Hotel stays are covered up to 600 AED per night inside the UAE.
Meals during business travel are covered up to 150 AED per day.

## Not covered

Alcohol, fines and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.
