IT Handbook

Devices
Laptops are issued on your first day and must be re-
turned when you leave the company.
Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk
within 1 hour.
Company data must never be stored on personal USB drives
or personal cloud accounts.

Accounts and passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are
changed every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication is required for email, VPN
and the HR portal.
