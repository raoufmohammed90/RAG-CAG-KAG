    KAG

    Two flows: build once, then ask

    Each stage hands its output to the next one.

    Buildonce, ahead of time
    SchemaDocuments→Chunks→Facts→Aligned facts→Graph
    Askon every question
    Question→Plan→Follow edges→Read the text→Deduce→Answer
    Build · stage 1

    Decide the schema, gather the knowledge

    Two things happen in parallel.

    The schema written by domain experts

    HR and the policy owners decide what the graph may contain.
    Types of things
    Person Team Role Leave rule Policy
    Relations allowed between them
    Person works in Team
    Team managed by Person
    Person holds Role
    Role is a Role
    Leave rule part of Policy

    The knowledge collected from where it lives

    XLSXStaff listOmar Khalid · Sales team · since 2023
    PDFOrg chartSales team · managed by S. Ahmed
    XLSXHR directorySara Ahmed · Head of Sales
    PDFLeave policy§4 Carried-over days need the line manager's approval
    No single document answers "who approves Omar's carry-over?"
    Build · stage 2

    Chunk the documents

    As in RAG. The chunks are kept: every fact will point back to one.

    The leave policy, cut where its sections change
    Leave policy
    3. Annual leave 25 days a year; pro rata for part-time staff.
    4. Carry-over Up to 5 days. Carried-over days need the line manager's approval.
    5. Sick leave Report on the first day.
    6. Forms Use form HR-17, signed by the HR director.
    One chunk from each document
    Staff list Omar Khalid joined the Sales team in March 2023.
    Org chart Sales team: managed by S. Ahmed.
    HR directory Sara Ahmed, Head of Sales.
    Leave policy §4 Carried-over days need the line manager's approval.
    52 chunks in all
    Build · stage 3

    Send each chunk, with the schema, to the LLM

    The LLM writes down the facts it finds, in the schema's shape.

    Sent
    Instruction Extract the entities and relations in this text. Use only these types: Person, Team, Role, Leave rule, Policy. Use only these relations: works in, managed by, holds, is a, part of.
    Chunk "Omar Khalid joined the Sales team in March 2023."
    Returned
    Omar Khalid Person
    Sales team Team
    Omar Khalidworks inSales team
    The same prompt for every chunk
    Sales teammanaged byS. Ahmed
    Sara AhmedholdsHead of Sales
    Carry-overpart ofLeave policy
    "Needs the line manager's approval" is a condition, not a link between two things. It stays in the text.
    Build · stage 4

    Align: merge duplicates, link to concepts

    Raw facts are messy. Alignment makes them one consistent graph.

    The raw facts
    Omar Khalidworks inSales team
    Sales teammanaged byS. Ahmed Sara Ahmed
    Sara AhmedholdsHead of Sales
    Carry-overpart ofLeave policy
    Head of Salesis aLine manager
    Same entity? "S. Ahmed" (manages the Sales team) and "Sara Ahmed" (Head of Sales)
    Yes. Same team, same role: merge.
    Which concept? Is "Head of Sales" a kind of any Role in the schema?
    Yes. Head of Sales is a Line manager.
    Candidates come from similar names and meanings; the LLM confirms.
    Build · stage 5

    Store the graph, linked to its text

    KAG calls this mutual indexing: each node knows its chunks, each chunk its nodes.

    1

    Nodes with typesEach fact's two ends become nodes: a Person, a Team, a Role.

    2

    EdgesEach fact's relation becomes an edge between them.

    3

    Links to the textDashed tags: the chunk each node came from.

    4

    Searchable chunksIndexed by words and meaning, as in RAG, for when the graph has a gap.

    Built

    The graph is ready

    Built once, ahead of time. A changed document is extracted and aligned again.

    ✓

    Facts, not passagesWho works where, who manages whom, which role is which.

    ✓

    Each with its sourceSo answers can cite the document behind every step.

    →

    Now, a questionThe build pipeline folds into one box: the graph it produced.

    Ask · stage 1

    A question arrives

    The app also knows who is asking.

    Omar Khalid, signed inWho approves my carry-over request?
    "my" = Omar Khalid
    A node in the graph: the starting point.
    Plain RAG would search for text that looks like the question. KAG first works out what to look up.
    Ask · stage 2

    The LLM turns the question into a plan

    Small steps, each one an operation the app can run.

    Sent
    Question Who approves my carry-over request? Asked by Omar Khalid
    Operators Retrieval, Sort, Math, Deduce, Output Schema types and relations
    Returned: a plan in logical form
    1Retrieval(Omar Khalid, works in, ?team)
    2Retrieval(?team, managed by, ?manager)
    3Retrieval(Carry-over, approved by, ?role)
    4Deduce(?manager is a ?role) → Output(?manager)
    Retrieval, Sort, Math and Deduce let KAG answer "who", "how many" and "the most", which similarity search can't.
    Ask · stage 3

    Run the plan: follow the edges

    Steps 1 and 2 are lookups in the graph. Nothing is guessed.

    No LLM here. A graph lookup, like a database query.
    Ask · stage 4

    A missing edge? Read the linked text

    The graph has no "approved by". Carry-over points to its chunk.

    Sent to the LLM Who approves carry-over? Leave policy §4 "Carried-over days need the line manager's approval."
    Returned The line manager.
    Ask · stage 5

    Deduce: put the facts together

    Step 4 of the plan connects the two halves.

    Sara Ahmed holds Head of Sales.
    Head of Sales is a Line manager.
    Carry-over is approved by the Line manager.
    So Sara Ahmed approves Omar's carry-over.
    Ask · stage 6

    The LLM writes the answer, with the path

    Every step carries its source document.

    Facts Omar works in Sales team; Sales team managed by Sara Ahmed; Sara holds Head of Sales, a Line manager; carry-over needs the line manager. Question Who approves my carry-over request?
    Returned Sara Ahmed, your line manager, approves it.
    Sara Ahmed, your line manager, approves your carry-over request.
    You→ works inSales team→ managed bySara Ahmed· Line manager
    Sources: Staff list · Org chart · HR directory · Leave policy §4
