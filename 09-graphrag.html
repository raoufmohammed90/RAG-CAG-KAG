    GraphRAG

    Two flows: build once, then ask

    Microsoft Research, 2024. Built for questions about a whole collection.

    Buildonce, ahead of time
    Documents→Text units→Entities→One graph→Communities→Reports
    Askon every question
    Question→Map over reports→Reduce→Answer
    Build · stage 1

    Gather the knowledge

    Three years of employee feedback. No schema is designed.

    The files

    CSVFeedback 20246,800 comments
    CSVFeedback 20256,900 comments
    CSVFeedback 20266,300 comments
    20,000 comments in all

    What they look like

    "The VPN drops in every client call."
    "Overtime every week since the merger."
    "Nobody can tell me what a promotion takes."
    "Parking is impossible after nine."
    Unlike KAG, nobody designs a schema first. The LLM decides what the entities are, from a short list of general types.

    Build · stage 4

    Merge into one graph

    The same entity, found in many units, becomes one node. The LLM sums up its descriptions.

    "VPN" was found in 312 text units
    VPN: drops during client calls
    VPN: needs a new login every hour
    VPN: very slow from home
    VPN: blocked at a client's site
    VPN: + 308 more descriptions
    VPN The remote-access tool. Staff report drops during client calls, hourly logins and slow speeds from home. (312 mentions)
    Every entity and relationship, merged the same way: about 1,800 entities and 4,000 relationships here (illustrative).
    Build · stage 5

    Groups of nodes that link to each other more than to the rest. No LLM: an algorithm (Leiden).

    Ask · stage 1

    A question about everything

    No single passage answers it.

    HR directorWhat are the main complaints in three years of feedback?
    Plain RAG would bring back 5 of 20,000 comments. KAG has no single path to follow.
    GraphRAG answers from the community reports, which already cover everything.
    Ask · stage 2

    Map: ask every report the question

    Separate LLM calls, in parallel. Each returns points with a helpfulness score from 0 to 100.

    In practice, reports are packed into batches that fill the context window, one call per batch.

    Ask · stage 4

    The big picture, from everything

    Built from summaries of the whole collection, not from a few similar passages.

    The main complaints, in order of weight:
    1. Workload: overtime and staffing gaps since the merger.
    2. Remote-work tools: VPN drops in client calls; IT takes about a week.
    3. Career growth: unclear promotion criteria.
    4. Pay and benefits: transparency and bonus dates.
    Based on 4 community reports, each traceable to its comments.
    The other mode

    Local search: one entity and its neighbours

    For specific questions, GraphRAG starts from a matching entity instead.

    Team leadWhat do people say about the VPN?
    1. Find the entity: VPN, by comparing the question with entity descriptions.
    2. Add its neighbours: IT support, Client calls, Logins, Laptops.
    3. Add the text units that mention it, and its community's report.
    Answer It drops during client calls and asks for a new login every hour; IT tickets about it take around a week.
