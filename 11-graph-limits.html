The price of KAG and GraphRAG.

    1. Costly to build

    LLM calls before the first question, for the same 8 chunks.

    The GraphRAG repository: "indexing can be an expensive operation… start small". KAG paper: "multiple LLM calls during the construction and solving phases". Cheaper modes exist (LazyGraphRAG, KAG's lightweight build).

    When a graph is worth it

    Build a graph when

    • ✓answers depend on relationships, rules or numbers
    • ✓people ask about the whole collection
    • ✓the knowledge is stable enough to rebuild in batches
    • ✓a wrong answer is costly, and you need its path

    Stay with RAG or CAG when

    • →one or two passages hold the answer
    • →the documents change all the time
    • →nobody can own a schema
    • →a cited, good-enough answer is fine
