10 KAG vs GraphRAG Agenda

KAG vs GraphRAG

Same start, different goals.

    The same start

    Documents
    →
    An LLM extractsentities and relations
    → KAGa precise graph, followed step by step
    → GraphRAGa map of themes, read as summaries

    What each one builds

    KAG

    Typed facts under a schema, each linked to its text

    GraphRAG

    Communities of entities, each with a written report

    How each one answers

    "Who approves Omar's carry-over?"

    One exact answer, by following a path

    "What are the main complaints?"

    The big picture, by merging summaries

    Which one answers this?

    Who approves Omar's carry-over request?
    KAG
    What are the main complaints in three years of feedback?
    GraphRAG
    How many teams average more than 10 unused leave days?
    KAG
    What themes come up in exit interviews?
    GraphRAG
    How do I set up the VPN on a new laptop?
    Plain RAG
    Connected facts, rules or numbers: KAG. Themes across everything: GraphRAG. One passage holds the answer: plain RAG is enough.
